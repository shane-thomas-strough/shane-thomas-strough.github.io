The Builder

The career started at the Naval Nuclear Power Training Command, where zero-tolerance discipline in physics, chemistry, and mechanics became the operating standard. That foundation carried through 15+ years of progressively complex field execution — managing up to 70-man crews across petrochemical, offshore, and federal infrastructure. At Blue Origin's LC-36 Vehicle Access Tower at Cape Canaveral, that meant restoring FAA compliance (AC 70/7460-1M) at 586ft with high-angle specialized access in a marine salt environment. Today, as Founder of Elevare Edge LLC, that same discipline drives the architecture of local-first AI infrastructure, quantitative trading systems, and enterprise-grade technology frameworks for high-trust SMBs.

The Founder

Elevare Edge LLC is the parent company — architecting local-first, secure AI ecosystems for high-trust SMBs across legal, medical, and industrial sectors. Elevare Scribe extends that mission into AI-assisted content and knowledge architecture. Both ventures are rooted in the same principle: sovereign systems, no dependencies on external APIs, and enterprise-grade security by default. The FOUND program, designed for a $2B+ enterprise, projected $1.5M+ in SBU-level ROI through AI-powered workforce development and targeted turnover reduction.

The Human

Father of three sons. Advanced Open Water diver (SSI) in the waters off El Salvador. Surfer — from Hurricane Gustav swells on St. Jose Island to the unnamed point break at K60. Poet and songwriter, currently writing from El Salvador. The same intensity that drives zero-downtime cutovers extends to every part of life — from the lineup to the page.

The Navigator

Having managed operations and lived across multiple continents — from Germany to Latin America, from the Scottish Highlands to the English coast — adaptability is not aspirational, it is operational. Dungeness crab fishing off Oregon, cycling the Rhine, snorkeling the Bahamas, exploring Tintagel with the boys. The world is not a destination; it is the operating environment.
