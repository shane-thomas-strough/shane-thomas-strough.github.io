Short Paragraph

Shane Thomas Strough is a director-level Integration and Commissioning Leader with over 15 years orchestrating complex, multi-discipline infrastructure programs across DoD/federal facilities, aerospace launch infrastructure (Blue Origin LC-36), hyperscale data centers, and industrial plants. He is the founder of Elevare Edge LLC, an AI security and infrastructure consulting firm, and Elevare Scribe, an AI-powered music performance platform. A Naval Nuclear Power Training Command graduate, Advanced Open Water certified diver, songwriter, and poet, Shane brings a rare combination of engineering discipline and creative depth to everything he builds.

Full Bio

Florida-based integration and commissioning leader with 15+ years executing complex, multi-discipline programs across DoD/federal facilities, aerospace infrastructure, industrial plants, and hyperscale data centers. From Blue Origin launch towers to the next generation of AI infrastructure — live environments stay online, schedules hold, and systems get commissioned right the first time.

Founder, Elevare Edge LLC. Builder of systems — electrical, digital, and human. When not architecting zero-downtime cutovers, his focus is on family and navigating the world. Having managed operations and lived across multiple continents — from the EU to Latin America — he has built a lifestyle around adaptability, resilience, and the ability to execute complex programs from anywhere on the map.
